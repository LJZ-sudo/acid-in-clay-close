## Selected Mechanism
Confined acid-water network with VTF-governed hybrid hopping: H1 is the best working mechanism because it matches the strongest and most global signal in the dataset: conductivity across 39/40 samples is better described by VTF than by Arrhenius or piecewise fits, indicating that proton mobility is governed primarily by temperature-dependent structural relaxation of a confined phosphoric-acid/water hydrogen-bond network rather than by a single fixed activation barrier. This is also chemically plausible for an attapulgite-confined acid-water system, where proton transport is expected to involve mixed structural diffusion and vehicular contributions within a continuous but nanoconfined hydrogen-bond network. The positive dependence of conductivity on loading N, much stronger than on acid/water ratio R, is consistent with transport being controlled mainly by how much connected mobile confined liquid network exists, not by a purely interfacial-only pathway. The observed low-temperature breakpoints and higher low-T apparent activation energies are then interpreted not as evidence that the dominant mechanism changes to a different carrier population, but as a crossover in the same confined network when structural relaxation slows enough that the conductivity curve becomes piecewise over a limited window. H1 therefore best captures the dominant transport physics while still allowing secondary low-temperature resolvability effects in EIS.
